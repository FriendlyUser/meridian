Meridian — Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026. Applies to the Meridian app on the web and on Android (io.github.friendlyuser.meridian).

The short version

Meridian does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

What stays on your device

Your tasks, notes, events, habits, focus history and settings are kept on your own device, in the app's (or your browser's) local storage. They are never sent to the developer or to anyone else. Deleting the app's data, or clearing the site's data in your browser, removes them.

Notes you lock are encrypted on your device with a key made from your passcode. The passcode is not stored and cannot be recovered by anyone.

A backup file is created only when you ask for one, and goes only where you choose to save or share it.

On Android, if you have device backup turned on, Android may include the app's data in the backup it keeps in your own Google account, so that it returns when you reinstall the app or move to a new phone. That backup is made by Android and governed by Google's privacy policy; the developer has no access to it.

Permissions

The Android app asks to show notifications only if you turn on reminders. Reminders are scheduled on the device itself.

Network use

The Android app runs entirely offline. It asks for the Internet permission only because the system component that displays the app requires it; the app does not contact any server.

The web version is delivered by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address when your browser fetches the page; see GitHub's privacy statement. The app itself makes no other requests.

Children

Meridian collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions can be raised at github.com/FriendlyUser/meridian/issues.
